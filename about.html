Company Profile

Built to simplify cross-border trade.

SYLVA deals with importation and exportation of goods. It is a trusted Togo-based trading company established in 1995. for many decades, we have reliably supplied essential goods across industrial, construction, medical, and household sectors. Our diverse import portfolio includes caustic soda for manufacturing, canned sardines and tin tomato for food security, sterile medical syringes for healthcare, welding electrodes for fabrication, ceramic floor tiles and louvres for mordern building, plus durable corrugated roofing sheets for shelter, Built on integrity, quality, and timely delivery. SYLVA serves wholesalers, contractors, and retailers nationwide, bringing global supply with Togo's growing market needs. SYLVA IMPORTS & EXPORTS DJAGBLE, TOGO helps businesses source products, organize shipments, and connect with dependable supply channels.

We work with buyers, wholesalers, distributors, and project-based clients who need access to quality goods and straightforward coordination.

Sodium Lauryl Ether Sulfate
Chemical

Sodium Lauryl Ether Sulfate
(SLES) 70%

Sodium Lauryl Ether Sulfate (SLES) is an anionic surfactant derived from ethoxylated lauryl alcohol, followed by sulfation and neutralization. It appears as a viscous, clear to pale-yellow liquid or paste with mild odor. Known for excellent foaming, emulsifying, and cleansing properties, SLES is less harsh than SLS due to ethoxylation. It is widely used in personal care products like shampoos, body washes, liquid soaps, and toothpaste to create rich lather and remove oil and dirt. Also found in household cleaners, detergents, and car wash formulations. Industrials uses include textiles, agriculture, and firefighting foam. Handle with care to avoid eye/skin irritation.

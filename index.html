Caustic Soda
Chemical

Caustic Soda

Caustic soda, or sodium hydroxide, is a strong alkaline chemical used in soap making, paper production, textile processing, and water treatment. It is highly corrosive, dissolves grease and organic matter, and requires careful handling with protective gear due to burn risks. Meant For industrial and commercial supply requests.

Sardines
Food

Canned Sardines

Canned Sardines are small oily fish, usually canned in oil, water, or tomato sauce. It is rich in omega-3, protein, calcium, and vitamin D. Popular for long shelf life, affordability, and quick meals. Major export food supporting nutrition and food security in many regions. Suitable for wholesale and distribution needs.

Corrugated Roofing Sheets
Building

Corrugated Roofing Sheets

Corrugated roofing sheets are wavy metal or fiber panels used for roofing. The redges add strength and drain rainwater fast. Lightweight, durable, and cost-effective for homes, warehouses, and sheds. Often galvanized or coated to resist rust. Reliable supply for building and construction projects.

Medical Syringes
Medical

Medical Syringes

Medical syringes are sterile devices for injecting fluid or drawing blood. Made of clear plastic with precise volume markings and disposable needles. Essential in hospitals for vaccinations, drug delivery, and diagnostics. Single-use design prevents infection and cross-contamination. Available for supply inquiries and volume orders.
